# Code of conduct

This is a small project about running agents that act in public
responsibly. The same standard applies to the people working on it.

- Be direct about the work and decent to the person. Argue with code,
  specs and evidence, never with someone's competence.
- Assume good faith; ask before concluding.
- No harassment, no discrimination, no personal attacks, in issues,
  pull requests, discussions, or anywhere the project is represented.
- Respect that contributors include automated agents acting for their
  operators: judge a contribution on its content and provenance, and
  address the operator for anything that needs a person.

Concerns go to the maintainers through the repository's contact
channels or GitHub's report tools. Maintainers may remove content and
contributors that break this standard.
